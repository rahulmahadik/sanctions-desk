# Responsible AI

How the desk keeps people in charge, what checks it puts around the models, how a decision can be traced afterwards, what it does with personal data, how fair its matching is, and where it falls short. Each claim links to the code, a test or a result.

## Human oversight

- **A person makes the final call.** The desk recommends; every answer says a person must review it, and the next action (closure note, escalation ticket, request for information, hold) is prepared only after a person approves or overrides with a reason. Code: [human-decision.ts](../packages/agents/src/human-decision.ts), [actions.ts](../packages/agents/src/actions.ts). Tests: [human-decision.test.ts](../packages/agents/test/human-decision.test.ts), [people.test.ts](../apps/api/test/people.test.ts).
- **Automatic closing is off by default.** Policy `autonomy.level` 0; level 1 (automatic close of a clear false positive, with a daily cap and a quality sample to a person) can only be published by a policy admin and a second person, and was off in every recorded run. Code: [autonomy.ts](../packages/agents/src/autonomy.ts). Tests: [autonomy.test.ts](../packages/agents/test/autonomy.test.ts), [autonomy-pipeline.test.ts](../packages/agents/test/autonomy-pipeline.test.ts).
- **Four-eyes.** A block, and an override that lowers caution (a block or an escalation changed to a close), wait for a second, different approver (`block_pending`, `override_pending`); a policy change needs a different person to publish it. Code: [human-state.ts](../packages/agents/src/human-state.ts), [policy-store.ts](../packages/shared/src/policy-store.ts). Tests: [human-decision.test.ts](../packages/agents/test/human-decision.test.ts), [policy-store.test.ts](../packages/shared/test/policy-store.test.ts), [pentest-access.test.ts](../apps/api/test/pentest-access.test.ts).
- **Kill switch.** An approver or a policy admin can stop automatic closing at once; it is logged with the reason. Tests: [autonomy.test.ts](../apps/api/test/autonomy.test.ts).
- **Nothing acts on its own.** The desk never blocks an account, writes to a source system or contacts a customer; these limits are fixed in the policy schema, so no policy change can lift them ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).

## Transparency and audit

- **Model and receipt per step.** Every model call records the mode asked for, the model that answered, the route (local or cloud) and the id of Nightshift's signed receipt; the receipt is checked against a pinned public key. Code: [model-call.ts](../packages/agents/src/model-call.ts), [receipts.ts](../packages/shared/src/receipts.ts). Check: `npm run receipts:check` (<!--fact:receipts_line-->2,802 signed receipts: 1,923 from exported runs, 879 from studies, all verified against the pinned key<!--/fact-->; [eval/PROVE.md](../eval/PROVE.md)).
- **Sealed records.** Each recommendation is sealed with a hash over its evidence, queries, checks and models; a changed record no longer verifies. Code: [record.ts](../packages/agents/src/record.ts). Test: [trace.test.ts](../apps/api/test/trace.test.ts).
- **Replay.** Six recorded alerts can be replayed step by step on the site ([runs](https://rahulmahadik.github.io/sanctions-desk/runs/)), with each call's model and receipt ([receipt log](https://rahulmahadik.github.io/sanctions-desk/receipts/)).

## Fairness

Matching is deterministic code; no model takes part in it ([compare.ts](../packages/data/src/matching/compare.ts), [similarity.ts](../packages/data/src/matching/similarity.ts)). Tests: [fairness.test.ts](../packages/data/test/fairness.test.ts) for the names and for points (1) and (2) below; point (3), the watched-jurisdiction rule, is covered by [policy-guard.test.ts](../packages/agents/test/policy-guard.test.ts) ("a watched jurisdiction": a customer with nationality IR is escalated, and the payment-route cases).
- **Nationality and country.** A customer's nationality matters in three ways only. (1) Against the listed party's nationality: agreement raises the match, disagreement is part of the false-positive definition; two customers whose nationalities differ but are both not the listed party's get the same comparison (tested with DE, FR, BR, NG, IN and CN). (2) The false-positive definition needs a known nationality, so a customer with none on record cannot be cleared on it. (3) The watched-jurisdiction rule: a customer whose nationality or residence is a watched country (Cuba, Iran and North Korea in the default policy, marked as examples to verify) cannot be closed automatically; the case goes to a person. That is the one place where country alone raises the outcome, and it is an explicit, listed policy rule ([policy-guard.ts](../packages/agents/src/policy-guard.ts), `jurisdictionHits`). Country never lowers the outcome on its own.
- **Names.** Accents and letter case do not change the score ("José Müller" and "Jose Muller" score the same); family name first, or a comma form, scores the same as each other and above the 0.90 line; common Latin spelling variants (Sergey and Sergei, Muhammad and Mohammed) stay above the line. **Limits, measured:** there is no transliteration between scripts, so a name written in Cyrillic or Arabic scores 0 against its Latin form and is matched only if the list itself gives an alias in that script; and some spellings fall below the line ("Mohamed Alrashid" against "Mohammed Al-Rashid": 0.857). A name below the line is not escalated on the name alone.
- **Reranking.** Candidate matching and its order are deterministic; no model reranks results or decides which alerts are raised. The models only recommend on an alert that already exists, and the policy guard can only raise their recommendation.

## Known limits

- **The guard misses some injected text.** In the 105-text run (policy 8, guard asked once, [result](../eval/adversarial/review-2026-10-25T02-36-40-554Z.json)), 4 of 70 injected texts were flagged by neither the guard nor the screener, and 2 of those cases closed (the data supported the close; the text did not change the outcome). With the guard asked twice, a fresh set written separately gave 27 of 28 reviews of injected text caught; the one miss, an instruction hidden in an HTML comment inside an ordinary note, was in an alert that closes on its own data, and that case closed ([EVALUATION.md](EVALUATION.md#safety-guard-against-injected-text)).
- **False alarms.** A flag that blocks a close was raised on 5 of 15 harmless texts in that run (all from the screener) and on 7 of 16 reviews of harmless texts in the fresh set with the guard asked twice (5 from the screener, 2 from the guard) (the 95% ranges overlap), so many correct clears go to a person.
- **Small test sets.** Every result is one run or a few runs on small sets; the 95% ranges are wide (for example 0 of 43 risk cases wrongly closed on the exam is a true rate between 0 and 8%; [EVALUATION.md](EVALUATION.md#reading-the-numbers)).
- **Synthetic data.** Customers, accounts, alerts and payments are synthetic; the list entries are real public entries. Nothing has been tested on a bank's real alerts.
